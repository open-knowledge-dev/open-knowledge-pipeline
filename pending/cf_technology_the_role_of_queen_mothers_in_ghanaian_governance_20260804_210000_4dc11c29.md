**Introduction to Queen Mothers in Ghanaian Governance**

In Ghana, queen mothers are highly respected women who have been appointed or enstooled to serve as leaders in their communities. They are typically chosen based on their wisdom, integrity, and leadership abilities, and are often related to the paramount chief or king of the community. Queen mothers are responsible for advising the chief or king on matters of governance, mediating disputes, and promoting the welfare of their communities.
