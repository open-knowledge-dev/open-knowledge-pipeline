# The Moringa Plant And Its Medicinal Uses

In Ghanaian traditional medicine, Moringa is used to treat a wide range of ailments, from malaria and diabetes to arthritis and digestive issues. The leaves, which are rich in iron and calcium, are used to boost the immune system and promote healthy bone growth. The pods, which contain a sweet and slightly bitter pulp, are eaten raw or cooked, and are said to have anti-inflammatory properties.

Its deep roots allow it to tap into underground water sources, making it a drought-resistant crop that can survive in areas where other plants would wither and die.
