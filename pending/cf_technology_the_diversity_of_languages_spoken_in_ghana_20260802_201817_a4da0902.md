# The Diversity Of Languages Spoken In Ghana

Ghana, a country located in West Africa, is a melting pot of languages, with over 100 languages spoken across its territory. West Africa is a region characterized by linguistic diversity, with many countries having multiple languages spoken within their borders. While Ghana shares similarities with other West African countries in terms of linguistic diversity, there are also some unique differences. For example:
