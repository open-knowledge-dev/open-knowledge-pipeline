# Ghana'S Role In Pan-Africanism

3. **Regional cooperation**: Ghana has been a strong proponent of regional cooperation in West Africa, participating in organizations like the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) and the African Union (AU). This focus on regional integration is shared across West Africa, with countries like Nigeria, Senegal, and Côte d'Ivoire also prioritizing regional cooperation.

**Unique differences:**

1. **Kwame Nkrumah's leadership**: Ghana's role in Pan-Africanism is closely tied to the leadership of Kwame Nkrumah, who served as the country's first president from 1960 to 1966. Nkrumah's vision and charisma helped shape Ghana's Pan-Africanism, setting it apart from other West African countries. His influence extended beyond Ghana's borders, inspiring Pan-African movements across the continent.

This tradition of Pan-Africanism has continued to shape Ghana's identity and foreign policy.
