3. Bittersweet Vine (Catharanthus roseus): Used for treating various ailments, including hypertension, heart disease, and cancer, the bittersweet vine is a crucial medicinal plant in Ghanaian traditional medicine.

4. African Potato (Hypoxis hemerocallis): Also known as "Nsereame," the African potato is used to treat wounds, stomach ailments, and as a general tonic.

5. African Moringa (Moringa oleifera): Known as "dawadawa," "moringa," or "neogbe," this plant is used for treating a variety of health issues, including anemia, diarrhea, and skin conditions.
